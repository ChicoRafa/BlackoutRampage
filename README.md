# ⚡ BLACKOUT RAMPAGE

> **A time-trial shop management game inspired by Overcooked, set during the April 28th blackout**

## 🚀 About This Project

**Blackout Rampage** is a **frantic time-management game** where you run a convenience store during a major power outage. Inspired by **Overcooked's** cooperative chaos, this single-player experience challenges you to serve increasingly demanding customers while managing inventory, upgrades, and your sanity across three intense days.

## ⚡ Game Overview

### 📅 **Three-Day Campaign**
Navigate through **3 progressively challenging levels**, each representing a day during the blackout. Each day has specific **money and happiness targets** you must achieve to progress.

### 🏪 **Shop Management Mechanics**
- **Inventory System** - Manage 9-slot inventory with smart selection controls
- **Customer Service** - Serve diverse customers with unique behaviors and patience levels
- **Truck Deliveries** - Restock inventory with timed delivery system
- **Perk Shop** - Purchase upgrades between levels to improve your performance

## 🎯 Game Modes & Features

### 👥 **Dynamic Customer Types**
- **Karen** - Explodes and reduces everyone's patience by 30%
- **CryptoBro** - Provides bonus money when satisfied
- **Police Officer** - Gives happiness bonuses
- **Impatient Customer** - Constantly moving, low patience
- **Santa** - Restores everyone's patience as a Christmas gift
- **Regular Customers** - Various patience levels and behaviors

### 🛒 **Advanced Shop Systems**
- **Multi-tier Shelving** - Upgrade shelves to hold more products (up to 3 levels)
- **Strategic Inventory** - Products spawn on shelves and arrive via truck deliveries
- **Dynamic Pricing** - Balance profit margins with customer satisfaction
- **Interactive Computer** - Call delivery trucks on-demand (with upgrade)

### 🎵 **Immersive Audio Design**
- **Dynamic Music System** - Music affects customer patience
- **Contextual SFX** - Rich audio feedback for all interactions
- **Mixer Controls** - Separate volume controls for music and effects
- **Diegetic Audio** - Environmental sounds that enhance immersion

- **Modular upgrade system** allowing easy content expansion

### 🎨 **Professional Game Design**
- **Game Manager** controlling flow between menu, gameplay, and results
- **Save System** persisting progress and upgrades across levels
- **Dynamic UI** adapting to game state and input method

### 🧠 **Advanced AI Systems**
- **Customer Queue Management** with patience multipliers
- **Dynamic Behavior Trees** for each customer type
- **Real-time Economic Balance** adjusting difficulty and rewards
- **Power-up Integration** affecting global game state

1. **Day Preparation** - Purchase perks and upgrades in the shop
2. **Service Rush** - Manage customers, inventory, and deliveries
3. **Performance Review** - Check if money/happiness targets are met
4. **Progression** - Advance to next day or see final results
